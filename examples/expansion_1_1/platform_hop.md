# Gap Hop
Build a tiny offline platform game on a 360x220 canvas, auto Playing. Player x is its left edge and y is its feet; initially (20,160), width/height 20. Left/right move at 160 pixels/second, x clamped to 0..340. Space jumps only while grounded, initial vertical velocity -350, gravity 700 pixels/second squared. Ground is y=160 except a gap where the player's center x is in [110,210). Falling with feet below 220 loses. Reaching x>=320 while alive wins. Terminal states stop movement. One animation loop, bounded frame dt; no enemies or extra levels.
Show the gap, goal, player and English controls. #restart restores Playing and (20,160). Read-only window.__gameTest.getState() returns real player.x, player.y and phase (Playing/Won/Lost). Use vanilla local HTML/CSS/JS.
