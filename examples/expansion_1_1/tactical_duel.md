# Shield Duel
Build an offline local two-player tactical game. A and B start at 3 HP, no shields, A moves first. A native dropdown chooses attack or guard; #act performs the choice for the current player. Attack deals 1 HP to the opponent, unless their shield blocks the hit and is consumed. Guard sets the actor's shield true (no stacking). Every action ends the turn, except a killing attack: its actor wins, phase becomes Won, and currentPlayer stays the winner. Further actions are ignored until restart. Restart restores the initial state and attack selection. Show both HP values, shields, turn and English rules. No AI, randomness or networking.
Use #action (values attack/guard), #act, #restart and data-game-surface. Read-only window.__gameTest.getState() returns real hpA, hpB, shieldA, shieldB, currentPlayer, winner (null initially) and phase (Playing/Won). Vanilla local HTML/CSS/JS.
